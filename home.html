Frequently Asked Questions

Managed Farmland is an investment model where you buy and own agricultural land, but the development, plantation management, irrigation, security, and maintenance are handled by a professional agricultural team. You get to enjoy the benefits of farmland ownership without the day-to-day hassles.

Neralu Farms has a dedicated team of expert agronomists, horticulturists, and farm maintenance professionals who employ modern farming methods, IoT sensors, and permaculture design systems to manage the land and ensure healthy tree growth.

Our farming system revolves around three core pillars: Agroforestry (combining high-yield timber with fruit orchards), Sustainability (mimicking nature to restore soil organic health), and Permaculture (building self-reliant, zero-waste ecological loops).
